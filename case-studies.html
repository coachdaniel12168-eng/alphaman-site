Tech Scale-Up Saves 40% in Time-to-Hire

Industry: Enterprise SaaS
Challenge: A Series-C technology company needed to scale from 80 to 200 employees within 12 months. Manual screening was bottlenecking the process — average time-to-hire was 52 days.
Solution: AlphaMan's automated screening engine processed 1,200+ inbound applications, generating ranked shortlists for 35 open engineering and product roles.

Regional Manufacturing Firm Reduces Mis-Hire Rate by 60%

Industry: Industrial Manufacturing
Challenge: A regional manufacturer with operations across 3 SEA countries was experiencing a 25% mis-hire rate on middle-management positions, resulting in significant re-hiring costs and operational disruption.
Solution: AlphaMan deployed automated fit-scoring across 15 management roles, incorporating industry-specific competency matrices and cultural alignment indicators based on successful incumbent profiles.
Result: Mis-hire rate reduced to 10% within the first year (60% reduction). Retention of placed candidates at 12 months improved to 92%. Estimated cost savings of $380,000 in avoided re-hiring expenses.
