Engagement Phases

Phase 1 — Discovery & Mapping (Weeks 1–2)

In-depth consultation to define role requirements, compensation benchmarks, and target company mapping. Deliverable: Search Strategy Document with shortlisted target profiles.

Phase 2 — Outreach & Assessment (Weeks 3–6)

Confidential outreach to passive candidates. AI-powered screening combined with senior consultant assessment. Deliverable: Shortlist of 3–5 pre-vetted candidates with detailed evaluation reports.

Phase 3 — Selection & Closure (Weeks 7–10)

Interview coordination, compensation negotiation support, reference verification, and offer management. Deliverable: Signed acceptance and onboarding transition plan.

Phase 4 — Post-Placement Assurance (90 Days)

Regular check-ins with both client and placed executive. 12-month replacement guarantee if the placement does not work out within the first year.

Payment Terms

All fees processed securely through AIRWALLEX. Engagement retainer (one-third) due at search initiation. Balance due upon candidate acceptance. Invoicing available in SGD, USD, or EUR.
