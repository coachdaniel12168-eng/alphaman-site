The AlphaMan Automated Screening Engine

A multi-stage data pipeline that transforms raw candidate profiles into ranked, compatibility-scored shortlists — reducing time-to-shortlist by up to 80% compared to manual screening.

Data Pipeline Architecture

Stage 1: Resume Parsing

Proprietary NLP engine extracts structured data from unstructured resume formats — work history timeline, skill taxonomy, certification registry, education credentials, and language proficiencies. Handles PDF, DOCX, and LinkedIn export formats.

Stage 2: Skill Gap Analysis

Extracted skills are mapped against the target role's required competency matrix. The engine identifies exact matches, adjacent skills, and gap scores across 40+ dimensions including technical proficiency, domain expertise, and leadership indicators.

Stage 3: Automated Fit Scoring

A composite compatibility score is generated weighting technical match (50%), experience relevance (25%), industry alignment (15%), and career trajectory (10%). Scores above 85 trigger automatic shortlist inclusion; 70-84 are flagged for recruiter review.

Stage 4: Human Verification

Shortlisted profiles undergo mandatory senior consultant review before client delivery. AI scoring provides the data foundation; human judgment validates fit quality, cultural alignment, and outlier assessment.

Data Privacy

All candidate data is processed within AlphaMan's secure infrastructure. Profiles are anonymized before client delivery. Candidates explicitly approve profile release for each individual opportunity. Full compliance with PDPA and regional data protection regulations.
