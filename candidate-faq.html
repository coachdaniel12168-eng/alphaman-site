Frequently Asked Questions

How does resume parsing work?

AlphaMan's NLP engine extracts structured data from your submitted resume: employment history timeline, skill taxonomy, certifications, education credentials, and language proficiencies. This data populates your anonymized candidate profile used for matching. The parsing process is automated and typically completes within 5 minutes of submission.

How is my data protected?

Your profile data is stored in AlphaMan's encrypted infrastructure. Your identity and current employer information remain confidential. Your anonymized profile is only shared with hiring organizations after you explicitly approve each individual match. Full compliance with Singapore's PDPA and regional data protection regulations is maintained at all times.

What is the typical response timeline?

After profile submission, our AI engine begins matching against active requisitions immediately. Initial match results are typically available within 48 hours. If a match is identified, you receive a notification with role details. The full recruitment cycle — from match notification to offer acceptance — averages 3-6 weeks depending on role complexity and interview scheduling.

What interview preparation is provided?

When a match is confirmed, AlphaMan provides you with: a detailed role brief including team structure and reporting lines, the hiring organization's interview format and assessment criteria, and suggested preparation topics based on the specific requirements of the position. For executive-level placements, a dedicated consultant provides interview coaching and feedback throughout the process.

What criteria determine my profile matches?

Our matching engine evaluates five primary dimensions: technical skill alignment with role requirements, professional experience relevance, industry and domain adjacency, career progression trajectory, and certification/credential validation. The composite match score determines shortlist inclusion. Your profile can match multiple positions simultaneously, and you retain full control over which opportunities to pursue.
